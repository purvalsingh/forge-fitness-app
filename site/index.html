How it works

Three things. That's the whole app.

Eat

Search “poha”, “rajma chawal” or “dosa” and tap. FORGE knows Indian portions, so you never weigh anything.

  • Katori, roti, plate sizes built in
  • On Android, just say it: “do roti aur dal”
  • Snap a photo or scan a barcode

Train

Pick a ready plan or build your own. FORGE tells you what to lift today and times your rest.

  • Plans for gym or home
  • Rest timer that buzzes
  • Weights go up as you get stronger

See progress

Weight, steps and water on one page. Steps count by themselves with your phone in your pocket.

  • Calorie target that learns from you
  • Water tile that fills like a glass
  • Streaks that keep you going

Make it yours

Six gradients. Even the icon changes.

Pick a gradient and the whole app follows, right down to the icon on your home screen. Tap one to try it here.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no paid plan.

Android can't install the app. What do I do?

Android is careful with apps from outside the Play Store. When it asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, in Safari. Open the browser version, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like a normal app.

Do I need internet?

Only to sync. You can log food and workouts offline; they save when you're back online.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Open the app and choose to try it without an account. Your data stays on that phone.
