SHEET 04 — TOLERANCE CHECK

Tell it you ate a nuclear bomb. It won't log it.

Every entry is checked twice. First, anything that isn't food, an impossible amount (40 rotis at once) or an attempt to trick the AI is refused. Then the AI's answer is checked against basic food science: no food has more than 9 calories per gram, and the protein, carbs and fat must add up to the calories. If the numbers don't make sense, they never reach your diary.

SHEET 05 — SECTION A–A THROUGH YOUR KEYS

Your keys. Locked.

  1. 1 · BANK-GRADE ENCRYPTION

    Your 2–5 Gemini keys are locked with AES-256 encryption, the same standard banks use. The key that unlocks them never touches the database, and each one only works for your account.

  2. 2 · NEVER SHOWN AGAIN

    After you save your keys, the app only ever sees the last four characters. The browser doesn't hold them, and neither does the APK.

  3. 3 · YOUR DATA IS YOURS

    The database itself enforces that only you can read your meals, workouts and keys. Even a bug in the app couldn't show them to someone else.

  4. 4 · SPEED LIMITS ON AI

    Each account can make only so many AI requests per minute and per day. If someone ever got into your account, they couldn't burn through your free quota.

  5. 5 · LOCKED-DOWN WEBSITE

    Always HTTPS. The site can't be embedded in other sites or load code from anywhere else. Food photos go straight to the AI and are never stored.

  6. 6 · SIGNED APP

    Every Android release is signed with the same FORGE key, and its fingerprint (SHA-256) is printed on this page so you can check your download wasn't tampered with.

SHEET 07 — NOTES

Questions

N1 Is FORGE free?

Yes. The AI runs on your own free Gemini keys, so there are no subscriptions and no ads.

N2 Why 2–5 Gemini keys?

Each free key has a quota. FORGE rotates between your keys, so the food camera and coach keep working on heavy days. Keys from different Google projects give you more room.

N3 How accurate is the calorie count?

Dishes in the database are calculated from their ingredients at typical serving sizes. AI photo estimates are checked against that database and shown as editable estimates, never as facts. Portions are the hardest part, so adjust with − / + before you log.

N4 Does it work offline?

Yes. The app and its food and exercise data live on your phone. Entries sync when you're back online.

N5 Can I delete my data?

Yes. Delete your AI keys any time in Settings, and export everything as JSON or CSV.
